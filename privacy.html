PRIVACY POLICY — ANIMO

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Animo ("the App") collects, how it's used, and your choices regarding that information.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Account Information

Animo creates an anonymous account for you automatically when you first open the app, no sign-up, email, or personal details are required to play. If you choose to link your account (via "Sign in with Apple" on iOS, or Google Sign-In on Android, an optional feature used to back up and restore your progress on a new device), we receive the identifier that provider gives us for that sign-in.

Step and Motion Data

Animo uses your device's built-in motion sensor and activity-recognition capabilities (Apple's Core Motion framework on iOS, or the Android Activity Recognition API on Android) to count your steps. Step counts are used entirely to power in-app gameplay, hatching eggs, leveling up companions, and earning in-game currency. We do not collect your location, route, or GPS data at any point.

Camera and Augmented Reality (AR) Data

To power AR features, such as projecting your companions into the real world for photo capture and interactions, the App requires access to your device's camera. Your camera feed and spatial environment data are processed strictly locally on your device in real-time. We do not record, store, or transmit your camera feed, captured photos, or environmental mapping data to our servers.

Purchase Information

If you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by the app store you downloaded Animo from (Apple's App Store or Google Play) and our payment partner, RevenueCat. We receive confirmation of what was purchased and your anonymous account identifier so we can grant the correct in-game rewards. We do not receive or store your payment card details, those are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

Usage and Analytics Data

We use PostHog, a third-party analytics service, to understand how the app is used in aggregate (for example, which features are used most, or where players tend to stop playing) so we can improve the game. This data is tied to your anonymous account identifier, not your real name or contact details.

Crash and Error Reports

We use Sentry, a third-party error-monitoring service, to automatically receive reports when the app crashes or encounters an error, so we can fix bugs. These reports may include technical details about your device (model, OS version) and the state of the app at the time of the error.

Push Notification Data

If you enable notifications, we store a device-specific notification token so we can send you reminders about your companion's care needs or your daily streak. You can disable notifications at any time in the app's Settings or your device's system settings.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information above solely to:

  • Operate and maintain core gameplay features
  • Enable augmented reality (AR) features and interactions (processed locally)
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Diagnose and fix bugs and crashes
  • Understand aggregate usage patterns to improve the app
  • Send optional care and streak reminder notifications
  • Back up and restore your progress, if you choose to link an account

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Animo relies on the following third-party services, each of which processes certain data on our behalf under their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase (account authentication and game data storage)
  • RevenueCat (in-app purchase processing)
  • Apple (App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple, push notifications)
  • Google Play (Google Play purchases, Sign in with Google, push notifications)
  • PostHog (usage analytics)
  • Sentry (crash and error reporting)

4. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app, under Settings > Account Management > Delete Account & Start Over. This immediately erases your companions, progress, and purchase history from our servers and signs you out. This action cannot be undone.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Animo does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described in this policy (an anonymous account identifier and gameplay data). We do not require or request a name, email address, or other personally identifying information to play, unless you voluntarily choose to link an Apple ID.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. Since Animo does not collect personally identifying information by default, most of these rights can be exercised directly through the in-app account deletion feature described above. For any other request, contact us using the details below.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
