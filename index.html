Your Steps, Brought to Life.

Turn your everyday walks into a cozy AR pet-collecting adventure. Hatch, grow, and play with over 80 unique companions that react to your real-world movement.

[ Download Now (Available on iOS & Android) ]

Animo is a fitness-based pet collection game where your physical movement powers your virtual world. There are no arbitrary wait timers and no pretending you walked when you didn't. Whether you are building a daily walking habit, taking the dog out, or exploring a new city on vacation, every step you take helps your companions grow. If you want to see your Animo evolve, you just have to go outside and move.

Core Features

Walk to Hatch & Grow

Your first egg hatches in just 200 steps. From there, your real-world movement levels up your active companion and earns Kibble to keep them fed and happy.

Collect Over 80 Companions

Discover eight unique lineages, including Chicks, Bears, Beavers, Koalas, Penguins, Capybaras, Cats, and the legendary Elemental line. Track your Common, Rare, Epic, and Legendary discoveries in your Codex.

Choose Their Destiny

Your Animo doesn't just get bigger; it grows into who you decide it becomes. Will your Teen become an artist or a scientist? Every companion's story branches based on your choices.

Build Your Sanctuary

Decorate a free-roaming zen garden with trees, lanterns, and ponds using the currency you earn from walking. Watch your fully-grown companions wander and rest in the space you built.

Step Into Their World

Animo features a fully integrated Augmented Reality suite, bringing your pets directly into your living room or local park.

  • Magical Entrances: Tap a physical surface to drop your Animo into the real world with their signature umbrella-parachute entrance.
  • Realistic Presence: Dynamic room lighting and soft contact shadows keep your pet grounded in your environment.
  • Interactive Play: Drop a bowl of kibble on your real-world floor to feed them, swipe to play a game of fetch, or scatter hidden crystals around your room for an AR scavenger hunt.
  • AR Photo Mode: Pose your pets, choose a custom decorative frame, and capture the perfect shot of your Animo in the real world.

Global Community

Developed by Aizu Labs. Fully playable in English, Japanese, Korean, and Traditional Chinese. Join a global community of walkers building healthier habits one step at a time.
